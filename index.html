01 / The cost of knowing, and not doing

You already know AI is the answer. So why are you still here?

You don't need to be told AI changes everything. You already know. What you don't have is someone who comes into your business, finds the exact places you're losing time and money, and builds the system that fixes it — while teaching you to run it yourself.

    Time you'll never get back. Repetitive tasks, manual offering, bureaucracy. The work that eats tens of hours every week and never moves the business forward.

  2. ii

    Decisions made in the dark. No clear dashboard of leads, sales, opportunities. You're guessing where the money goes and where the next client comes from.

  3. iii

    Clients slipping through the cracks. No follow-up system. Poor communication quality. Sales you already earned and then lost.

06 Before you write

What you're probably wondering.

Isn't this expensive compared to a $15 Udemy course?

Compared to a $15 course, yes. Compared to a $500/hour corporate consultant, no. We can split the frequency over a longer interval so the work fits your budget and your calendar. You'll see results from the first things we change — not after six months of theory.

I don't have time for courses.

Then you definitely don't have time to lose to competitors who are already using AI. We can space the sessions so they fit your week. But the real question is — what's it costing you every month you wait? Your competitors aren't waiting.

Can't I just learn this from YouTube for free?

You can learn anything from YouTube. But you'll spend tens of hours figuring out which of the thousands of videos actually fits your problem, then more hours implementing what you don't need. A consultant finds your exact problem and builds the mix that solves it fast. YouTube doesn't track your objectives, doesn't help on your projects, and doesn't keep you updated on what's new.

Does this even apply to my field? I'm a doctor / architect / lawyer / stylist.

There's no field that can't be improved with AI. Every field has content, workflows, processes, management, marketing (often up to 40% of activity), branding, presentation, images, video. If your work involves any of those — and it does — there's a system to build.

I've been burned by "AI experts" before. They taught theory, nothing changed.

The difference is what we build. Plans are made for your business, implemented step by step, tracked, adjusted. I've been coaching and teaching long enough to know — if nothing changed after you worked with someone, it's usually because nothing was actually done. Without involvement, no results. With me, you'll be involved, and you'll see the system.

Can one person really do branding, web, AI, and coaching?

I don't do it alone. I have a network of about eighty specialists — web design, content, branding, graphics, programming, sales — I've worked with for years. You get one point of contact, a full team behind it.

Price is "on request" — are you going to pressure me to buy?

No. You choose. Everything is transparent. One message, we talk, you decide. No one's going to chase you.
