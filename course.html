05 FAQ

Course questions.

Is this a recorded course or live?

Live. You join sessions, you ask questions, we work together. You can't get that from a recording.

What if I miss a session?

We find a way — a makeup session, a recording of that specific session, or notes. You don't fall behind.

Can I bring my team?

Yes. HR managers and team leads bring groups of employees. We can structure a dedicated track for your team.

Do I need to know AI already?

No. We start where you are. If you've never used an AI tool, that's fine. If you've been using them for a year, we go deeper.

What if I want to stop?

It's monthly. You can stop at the end of any month. No lock-in.
