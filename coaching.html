03 How we work

Four steps, in this order.

  1. i

    First message

    You message me on WhatsApp. We talk about your business, what's eating your time, what you want to grow.

  2. ii

    Plan

    I propose a plan: what we build first, what we track, how many sessions, what it costs. You decide.

  3. iii

    Build + teach

    We meet 1-on-1. I build the system in your business and teach you to run it. You start using it from week one.

  4. iv

    Track + adjust

    We watch what's working, fix what isn't, keep going. I stay close — for the wins and the blocks.

05 FAQ

Coaching questions.

How many sessions do I get?

Depends on the plan. We figure that out in the first message. Usually between 4 and 12 sessions over a few weeks to a few months.

What if I need help between sessions?

You can reach me. That's part of the plan.

Do you work with my industry?

If your work involves content, processes, marketing, clients, or presentations — yes. Doctors, architects, lawyers, coaches, stylists, freelancers, founders — all welcome.

What if I don't have technical skills?

You don't need them. I'm a programmer — I build the system. You learn to use it, not to code it.

What if I want to stop?

You can. The plan is agreed upfront. If you want to stop mid-plan, we settle what's been done. No lock-in.
