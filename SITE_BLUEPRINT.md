## 0. Context capsule (Agent 3 must read this first)

**What the business is:** ambru.cc is the personal brand of Ambru Cosmin Claudiu. He teaches solopreneurs, founders, top employees and small service-business teams how to use AI to eliminate repetitive work and grow. He does this through three offers: 1-on-1 coaching, weekly online courses, and a private membership group. A secondary offer is "build done-for-you" (brand + web + marketing + AI automations) for clients who don't want to learn.

**Who the audience is:** Two buyer personas. Persona A = practitioner (solopreneurs, founders, liberal professions — doctors, architects, engineers, lawyers, freelancers, personal brands, coaches, trainers, stylists) who buys for self. Persona B = HR/Manager who buys AI training for employees. Both already know they have a productivity/marketing problem, know AI is the solution, and seek guidance. They arrive from search, referral, social media, on mobile + desktop.

**Core problem:** They lose time (tens of hours/week), money (unproductive salaries, lost sales), opportunities, and clients because they don't know how to apply AI in their business — even though they know AI is the solution. Six problem themes: time/productivity, visibility/decisions, client relationship, brand/presentation, strategy, human resources.

**The offer:**
- Arm 1 — 1-on-1 Coaching: analysis + custom system development + teaching + tracking + block detection. Price on request. For solopreneurs, entrepreneurs, top employees, doctors, architects, engineers, freelancers.
- Arm 2 — Weekly Online Course: 1-2 sessions/week, 1-2 hours/day. Productivity + marketing + AI agents. From $20/month. For practitioners + employee groups.
- Arm 3 — Monthly Membership (private group): community + AI productivity learning + advice + news updates. Enrollment via WhatsApp. From $27/month. For solopreneurs, entrepreneurs, freelancers, small businesses, liberal professions.
- Secondary — Build done-for-you: brand + web + marketing + AI automations by Ambru + his network of ~80 expert consultants. Price on request, project-based.

**Primary CTA:** WhatsApp. **Secondary CTAs:** Facebook, LinkedIn.

**Tone with example phrases:** Friendly, modern, warm, encouraging, optimistic. A guide who is enthusiastic about education and genuinely happy to see others' results.
- "Let's see what we can build very fast so the impact is as large as possible."
- "Let's start with the most complex problems you have and find solutions from the first try."
- "I don't sell you a course — I help you see what you need, build your system, and teach you to use it, so you can then manage on your own and adapt fast to what's coming in AI."

**Desired perception:** Calm, in good hands, "I found someone who understands me", confident he'll reach results, someone will be there for both successes and blocks.

**Anti-ton (never sound like):** Instagram AI guru, corporate agency, generic Udemy course, desperate for clients, unprofessional, mercenary/money-chasing, rigid corporate that blocks free communication.

**Art direction inputs:** KEEP the existing design — dark background (#08080B), violet+coral gradient, Fraunces serif display + Inter Tight body + JetBrains Mono utility. Video hero stays. The current dark editorial direction deliberately breaks: AI guru sites (loud, neon, hype), corporate agency sites (cold, blue, sterile), Udemy-style course pages (white, generic, flat). Must not feel like a hype guru, a cold corporate agency, or a generic course platform. Register: calm/challenger. Context: mobile + desktop.

**Imagery route:** Existing assets (video hero, SVG icons, og-image) + own photography for new sections. No stock photos of isolated people on white, no handshake shots, no laptop-and-coffee flatlays, no stock smiles at conference tables.

**Language:** English.

8. **FAQ** — Objection handling. Nielsen's heuristics: visibility of system status — answer the unspoken "but what about..."
9. **Final CTA** — Serial position effect: last thing remembered. One CTA, large, WhatsApp.
