02 How I work

How I work.

Most AI training is theory you have to apply alone. Most agencies do the work and leave you unable to run it. I do the third thing — I build the system inside your business, teach you to use it step by step, and stay close while you learn. When I leave, you can run it yourself and adapt to what comes next.

I work 1-on-1, not in large groups. If you come in through a course or the membership, you still get 1-on-1 access when you need it. Every system I build is custom — not a template, not what worked for someone else. And I've been doing this long enough to see when a block isn't technical — sometimes it's a belief, a habit, a way of working that's been quietly sabotaging the result. I spot those too.
